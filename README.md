### ✨ About

Ksaevelo Tools is a free Adobe After Effects extension designed to make motion design faster, smoother, and more creative.

From animation utilities and creative presets to graph controls and 3D tools, everything is organized into one customizable workspace.

**Made with creativity. Built for creators. Free forever.**

### ⚡ Features

- **Animation & Layer Tools** — Speed up your everyday workflow.
- **Graph & Easing Tools** — Create smoother, more expressive animations.
- **Color & Text Presets** — Explore ready-to-use creative effects.
- **3D Presets & Materials** — Experiment with 3D-inspired motion tools.
- **Custom Themes** — Personalize your extension's appearance.
- **GitHub Update Notifications** — Stay informed about new releases.
